Registration clarity matters.

The company states that registration is used to filter serious candidates and is adjusted against the final consultancy service charges after an offer is received.

  • Access to available technical and non-technical opportunities
  • Mock interview sessions and preparation guidance
  • Relevant training material based on job requirements
  • Support throughout the recruitment journey
Registration or payment does not guarantee job selection. Final hiring depends on the employer's eligibility and recruitment process.
Fee Example

Simple explanation of the adjustment model.

Example shared by the consultancy: if a candidate pays ₹5,000 as registration and later receives an offer where the consultancy charge is ₹25,000, the registration amount is adjusted and only the remaining ₹20,000 is payable.

Consultancy service charge₹25,000
Registration already paid− ₹5,000
Remaining charge₹20,000
Always confirm the exact amount, written terms, cancellation/refund rules and job-specific conditions directly with the consultancy before making a payment.
