Registration & Fee Transparency

Understand the payment model before you register.

AS Career Consultancy states that registration is mandatory for technical and non-technical Work-from-Home opportunities and is used to filter serious candidates.

No placement guarantee

Registration is not the same as buying a job.

Final shortlisting, interview results and offer decisions remain with the employer's recruitment process.

Why AS says registration is used

To work with candidates who intend to complete the process.

The company lists issues such as candidates missing interviews, not joining after receiving offers, and not clearing agreed consultancy charges after an offer.

  • Eligibility to apply for available technical & non-technical opportunities
  • Mock interview sessions
  • Relevant training and interview-preparation material
  • Guidance throughout the recruitment process
