Service Philosophy

Preparation should make the candidate stronger, not dependent.

The purpose of guidance is to improve how a candidate approaches real recruitment. Final shortlisting, assessment results and job offers remain the employer's decision.

Registration or consultancy support should not be interpreted as a job guarantee. Confirm the exact written terms and applicable charges before payment.
